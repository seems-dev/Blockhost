## 3. Network and Security Abuse

You are strictly prohibited from using the Services to compromise the security or integrity of any network, computer system, or application. 

### 3.1 Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS)
You may not initiate, participate in, or facilitate any Denial of Service (DoS) or Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. This includes both inbound attacks directed at your Erex servers (which you deliberately provoke) and outbound attacks launched from your Erex servers against third-party targets. Furthermore, you may not host "booter" or "stresser" services.

### 3.2 Unauthorized Access and Probing
You may not attempt to gain unauthorized access to any system, network, or data. This includes:
- Port scanning, vulnerability scanning, or penetration testing without explicit prior written authorization from the target network's owner.
- Attempting to bypass or breach security authentication mechanisms.
- Accessing or intercepting data not intended for you (e.g., packet sniffing).

### 3.3 Malware and Malicious Code
You may not use the Services to create, host, distribute, or execute any malicious software, including but not limited to:
- Viruses, worms, trojan horses, rootkits, or spyware.
- Ransomware or associated command-and-control (C2) infrastructure.
- Exploit kits or malicious payloads.
- Software designed to stealthily alter the configuration of a user's computer.

### 3.4 IP Spoofing and Network Interference
You may not forge TCP/IP packet headers, email headers, or any part of a message describing its origin or route (IP spoofing). You may not interfere with the proper functioning of the internet by engaging in route hijacking or BGP manipulation.

Erex provides shared resources. You may not use the Services in a manner that disproportionately consumes resources and negatively impacts the experience of other users.

### 4.2 Artificial Traffic Generation
You may not use the Services to generate artificial web traffic, participate in traffic exchange networks, or operate click-fraud botnets.

You may not engage in continuous, intensive disk I/O operations that are unrelated to normal game server or application hosting operations. You may not use the Services primarily as an offsite backup repository, file-sharing node (e.g., public BitTorrent seedbox), or content delivery network (CDN) unless explicitly permitted by your specific subscription tier.

### 4.4 Intentional Circumvention
You may not attempt to circumvent any resource limits (CPU quotas, RAM limits, storage caps) imposed by the BlockHost infrastructure through exploitation of software bugs, nested virtualization, or any other technical means.
