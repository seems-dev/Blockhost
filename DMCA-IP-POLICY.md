# DMCA & Intellectual Property Policy

**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

**Sims Kushawaha**, operating as **Erex** and utilizing the **BlockHost** infrastructure (collectively "Erex", "we", "us"), respects the intellectual property rights of creators, developers, and copyright owners globally. We are committed to complying with applicable intellectual property laws, including the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and the Indian Copyright Act, 1957.

This policy outlines the strict procedures for reporting alleged intellectual property infringement occurring on servers or networks hosted by Erex, as well as the procedures for customers to file counter-notices.

---

## 1. Scope of Responsibility

Erex provides unmanaged, raw infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS). We do not actively monitor, curate, or control the specific files, mods, plugins, game server binaries, or websites that our customers choose to upload and host on their provisioned servers. 

Consequently, Erex acts as a mere conduit and hosting provider. Under standard safe harbor provisions globally, we are not liable for the infringing actions of our users, provided we act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the infringing material upon receiving a valid, legally compliant takedown notice.

## 2. Filing a Copyright Takedown Notice (DMCA / Indian Copyright Act)

If you are a copyright owner, or an authorized agent thereof, and you believe in good faith that a user on the Erex network is hosting content that infringes upon your copyrighted work, you must submit a formal, written notification to our Designated Copyright Agent.

To be legally valid and actionable, your takedown notice **must** include all of the following information:

### 2.1 Required Information for a Valid Notice
1. **Physical or Electronic Signature:** A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright that is allegedly infringed.
2. **Identification of the Original Work:** A clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed. If multiple copyrighted works are covered by a single notification, provide a representative list of such works.
3. **Identification of the Infringing Material:** A precise description of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, and that is to be removed or access to which is to be disabled. 
   - *Crucial:* You must provide sufficient information to permit us to locate the material. This requires providing the exact **IP Address**, specific **URLs**, and timestamps of the alleged infringement. Simply stating "a server on your network" is insufficient and will be rejected.
4. **Contact Information:** Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and a valid email address.
5. **Good Faith Statement:** A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. **Statement of Perjury:** A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

### 2.2 Designated Agent Contact

## 3. Erex's Actions Upon Receiving a Valid Notice

Upon receipt of a takedown notice that fully complies with the requirements outlined in Section 2, Erex will take the following steps:

1. **Expeditious Removal:** We will act expeditiously to disable access to, or remove, the specific material claimed to be infringing. Given the nature of server hosting, this may require temporarily null-routing an IP address or suspending the entire server if specific file-level access is technically unfeasible from the hypervisor level.
2. **User Notification:** We will promptly notify the Erex customer responsible for the server that we have removed or disabled access to the material, and we will provide them with a copy of the original takedown notice (including the complainant's contact information).
3. **Record Keeping:** We will log the incident against the customer's account for the purpose of enforcing our Repeat Infringer Policy.

## 4. Filing a Counter-Notice

If you are an Erex customer and you believe your server or content was suspended or removed due to a mistake, misidentification, or a fraudulent takedown notice, you have the legal right to submit a Counter-Notice.

To be legally valid, your Counter-Notice **must** include all of the following:

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature:** Your physical or electronic signature.
2. **Identification of the Material:** Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled, and the precise location (IP/URL) at which the material appeared before it was removed or disabled.
3. **Statement of Perjury:** A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled.
4. **Contact Info & Consent to Jurisdiction:** Your name, address, telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts located in India (or the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located, if outside India), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original takedown notice or an agent of such person.

Counter-Notices must be emailed to the Designated Agent at `seems.developer@gmail.com`.

### 4.1 Actions Upon Receiving a Counter-Notice
Upon receipt of a valid Counter-Notice, Erex will forward a copy to the original complainant. We will inform the complainant that we will replace the removed material or cease disabling access to it in 10 to 14 business days. 

Erex will restore the material within 10 to 14 business days following receipt of the Counter-Notice, **unless** our Designated Agent first receives notice from the original complainant that they have filed an action seeking a court order to restrain the customer from engaging in infringing activity relating to the material on Erex's network.

## 5. Trademark and Other IP Infringement

If you believe an Erex customer is infringing upon a valid Trademark or other intellectual property (non-copyright), please follow the same reporting procedure outlined in Section 2. You must provide your trademark registration number, jurisdiction, and proof of ownership. Erex will review trademark complaints on a case-by-case basis and take appropriate action in accordance with our Acceptable Use Policy.

## 6. Repeat Infringer Policy

Erex is committed to keeping its network free of persistent piracy. In accordance with the DMCA and industry standards, we have implemented a strict Repeat Infringer Policy.

- A "strike" is recorded against a customer's account each time Erex processes a valid, unchallenged takedown notice against their servers.
- Erex reserves the right to terminate the account, all active servers, and permanently ban any customer who accrues three (3) strikes within a 12-month period, or at our sole discretion based on the severity and scale of the infringement.
- No refunds will be issued for accounts terminated under the Repeat Infringer Policy.

## 7. Warning Regarding Fraudulent Notices

Please be aware that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, and equivalent laws globally, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing, or that material or activity was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, may be subject to severe civil liability and damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. We will not hesitate to pursue legal action against entities filing fraudulent or automated, inaccurate notices against our infrastructure.
