# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Blockhost is committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about the data we collect, store, and process when you use our Platform as a Service (PaaS).

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When you sign up, we collect your email address, username, and basic billing information required to maintain your account.

### B. Deployment Data
Because we are a hosting provider, you entrust us with your application data:
- **Environment Variables:** If you provide environment variables (like API keys or Database passwords) for your Web Apps, we encrypt these at rest in our database (`EncryptedJSON`). Our backend decrypts them only at the exact moment they are passed to the Docker Engine to boot your container.
- **Persistent Volumes:** Data stored in your application's persistent volume (like Postgres tables or user uploads) is stored securely on our underlying infrastructure (such as Amazon EFS). We do not index, search, or sell the contents of your databases or files.
- **Docker Images:** If you provide a private Docker registry URL, we store those credentials securely to pull your image.

### C. Telemetry and Logs
To maintain network stability and enforce fair-use limits, our Agents collect:
- **Resource Metrics:** Live CPU, RAM, and Disk Usage statistics of your Docker containers.
- **Network I/O:** Total bytes sent and received by your deployment.
- **Console Logs:** Standard Output (stdout) and Standard Error (stderr) logs generated by your web apps or Minecraft servers are temporarily cached so they can be displayed in your Blockhost Dashboard.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly to:
- Provide the hosting services you requested.
- Automatically rebalance nodes and orchestrate infrastructure.
- Enforce billing constraints and detect network abuse (e.g., detecting crypto-mining via CPU spikes).
- Troubleshoot errors or crashes on your specific deployments.

## 3. Data Sharing and Subprocessors

We do not sell your data. We only share necessary infrastructure metadata with trusted third-party cloud providers (Subprocessors) such as **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** and **Contabo**, who provide the underlying physical servers and network storage drives (EFS) where your data resides. Please see our `SUBPROCESSORS.md` for a complete list.

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your deployments. Traffic to our Control Plane and your custom domains is secured via SSL/TLS. However, you are responsible for implementing authentication and security within your own hosted applications and databases.

## 5. Deletion of Data

If you delete an App Deployment or Server from your dashboard, the background Agent is instructed to immediately stop the container and permanently delete your local persistent storage volume from the EFS drive. This action is irreversible.
