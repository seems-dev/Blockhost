# Erex Comprehensive Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

This Comprehensive Refund Policy ("Refund Policy") governs all financial transactions, subscriptions, and purchases made by you (the "User", "Customer", or "Subscriber") on **Erex**, powered by the **BlockHost** backend infrastructure. 

By purchasing any service from Erex, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund Policy in its entirety. This policy is designed to be highly specific to protect the operational integrity of the Erex platform.

---

## 1. The Principle of "Provisioned Resources"

Erex operates an automated hosting infrastructure. When you purchase a server or subscription, our systems immediately allocate computing resources (CPU, RAM, Disk Space, IPv4/IPv6 addresses) exclusively to your instance. 

Because these resources are immediately reserved for you and rendered unavailable for other potential customers, **all sales are considered final and non-refundable**, except under the strict, specific conditions outlined in this document. We do not offer "trial" refunds or "change of mind" refunds once the payment has been processed and the server has been provisioned.

## 2. Strict Non-Refundable Conditions

Under no circumstances will Erex issue a refund (full or prorated) in the following scenarios:

### 2.1 Unused Time and Early Cancellation
If you choose to cancel your subscription prior to the end of your billing cycle (e.g., canceling on day 10 of a 30-day billing cycle), you will **not** receive a refund for the remaining 20 days. Your server will remain active and accessible until the exact end of the billing period you have paid for.

### 2.2 Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) Terminations
If your account, server, or subscription is suspended or permanently terminated by Erex due to a violation of our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy (e.g., hosting malware, launching DDoS attacks, exceeding resource limits, or engaging in illegal activity), **all payments are strictly forfeit**. You will not receive any refund, and you remain liable for any damages caused to the infrastructure.

### 2.3 User Misconfiguration and Lack of Knowledge
We provide raw, unmanaged hosting infrastructure. We do not provide refunds because you lack the technical knowledge to configure your server, install plugins, manage game configurations, or operate Linux systems. If you accidentally delete your files, corrupt your database, or misconfigure a firewall leading to server inaccessibility, you are not entitled to a refund.

### 2.4 Third-Party Software Failures
Erex is not responsible for the stability, bugs, or performance issues of third-party software you choose to run on our infrastructure. We will not issue refunds for issues caused by:
- Buggy Minecraft server software (e.g., memory leaks in PaperMC, Spigot, Forge).
- Incompatible or resource-heavy plugins and mods.
- Issues with the Mojang authentication servers or third-party APIs.
- Game updates that break server functionality.

### 2.5 External Network and ISP Issues
We will not issue refunds if you are unable to connect to your server due to issues originating from your local Internet Service Provider (ISP), local network configurations, strict NAT routing, or localized internet outages outside of the AWS or Erex network boundary.

## 3. Conditions Eligible for a Refund

Erex will evaluate refund requests exclusively in the following limited scenarios:

### 3.1 Total Provisioning Failure
If you purchase a server, and due to a verified internal error within the BlockHost infrastructure, the server fails to provision entirely, and our support team is unable to manually provision or fix the server within **72 hours** of you opening a support ticket, you are entitled to a full refund of that specific transaction.

### 3.2 Verifiable Billing Errors and Duplicates
If our payment gateway (Razorpay) accidentally processes multiple identical charges for a single subscription period due to a system glitch on our end, we will refund the duplicate charge(s) in full. You must report this within **14 days** of the charge occurring.

### 3.3 Service Discontinuation
If Erex makes the business decision to permanently discontinue a specific service or server tier that you have already prepaid for (e.g., you paid for a year, and we shut down the service after 6 months), we will issue a prorated refund for the remaining, unfulfilled duration of the contract.

## 4. Subscriptions and Automatic Renewals

### 4.1 Consent to Recurring Billing
By purchasing a subscription, you explicitly authorize Erex and our payment processor (Razorpay) to automatically charge your saved payment method on a recurring basis (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually) according to the terms of your selected plan.

### 4.2 Responsibility to Cancel
It is your sole responsibility to manage your subscriptions. If you wish to stop recurring billing, you must cancel your subscription via the Erex billing dashboard **before** the renewal date. 

### 4.3 No Refunds for Forgotten Cancellations
If you forget to cancel your subscription and the automatic renewal is processed, **we will not refund the renewal charge**. The resources have already been allocated for the new billing cycle. You must cancel proactively.

## 5. Failed Payments and Account Suspension

If an automatic payment attempt fails (e.g., due to insufficient funds, expired card, or bank decline):
1. Erex will attempt to retry the payment over a standard grace period (typically 3 to 7 days).
2. During this grace period, your server may be suspended (shut down and inaccessible) until the payment is successfully captured.
3. If the payment remains unresolved at the end of the grace period, Erex reserves the right to permanently terminate the subscription and irreversibly delete the server and all associated data.
4. Erex is not liable for any data loss resulting from server termination due to non-payment.

## 6. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

### 6.1 Zero-Tolerance Chargeback Policy
We treat chargebacks, bank reversals, and PayPal/Razorpay disputes as severe violations of our Terms of Service, as they incur significant administrative costs and penalties for Erex.

### 6.2 Immediate Suspension
If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or credit card company for a valid charge, your entire Erex account, including all active servers, will be **immediately and permanently suspended** without notice, pending resolution of the dispute.

### 6.3 Administrative Fees and Legal Action
In the event of an unjustified chargeback, Erex reserves the right to charge a non-refundable administrative fee of up to ₹2500 INR (or equivalent local currency) per disputed transaction to cover our payment gateway penalties and labor costs. We further reserve the right to forward your account details, IP logs, and digital footprint to third-party collection agencies or pursue legal action in India to recover the stolen funds and associated fees.

### 6.4 Dispute Resolution First
If you believe you were charged in error, you must contact Erex Support first to attempt a resolution before contacting your bank. 

## 7. Force Majeure

Erex shall not be liable for any failure to provide the Services, and shall not be obligated to provide refunds or SLA credits, if such failure is caused by events beyond our reasonable control (Force Majeure). This includes, but is not limited to:
- Major outages at our primary cloud provider (Amazon Web Services).
- Global DNS or internet backbone failures.
- Natural disasters, fires, floods, or earthquakes.
- Acts of war, terrorism, or civil unrest.
- Government blockades, censorship, or regulatory shutdowns.
- Cyberattacks (such as massive upstream DDoS attacks) that exceed standard mitigation capabilities.
